# NABAT visual and interaction specification

The user delegated direction and implementation. The generated dashboard, mobile profile and three marketing-section concepts are the design reference, selected without an extra approval pause.

## Tokens and ownership

- Canvas: warm ivory `#f7f6ef`. Panels: `#fff`. Brand anchor/text: `#153d32`. Chlorophyll: `#a5c36b`; accessible success text: `#3d641d`. Saffron artwork: `#dc8c45`; action fill: `#ad571d` for white-text contrast. Critical: `#b63427`.
- Editorial: Fraunces, with Georgia fallback. Operations: Geist, with system sans fallback. Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic. Local font files, tabular numbers. 4/8 px spacing, 44 px minimum controls, logical CSS properties.
- Desktop shell: 232 px persistent green navigation; main 32–40 px gutter. Mobile: simple identity header, compact rows and five-item bottom navigation. Lists and tables remain operational; no marketing hero on management pages.
- Distinctive asset: segmented vitality arc. Health state, signed delta and confidence accompany it. Temporal motion only for NFC acquisition, confirmation, score arc and sheets. Reduced motion removes growth/reveal movement.
- Shared primitives: buttons, field labels/errors, status badge, plant image, vitality arc, trend chart, modal/dialog, compact plant rows, timeline.
- Brand mark: original SVG leaf/vein/radio geometry as explicitly requested. Botanical photographs are standalone generated sample assets, clearly synthetic. Actual user observations are private uploads.

## Screens and copy lock

Dashboard: “Good morning, Maya.” / “A little attention. A lot of life.” / “Add plant”; stats Total plants, Healthy, Need attention, Critical; Care starts here; Fleet vitality; Small care. Lasting change.; Today's rhythm; Your living collection. Numbers and names come from current workspace data.

Profile: plant name/scientific species/code/location; photo; arc/state/delta/confidence; Next care; Care, in one tap.; Watered/Fertilised/Repotted/Moved/Inspected; Add observation; A living history; Made for this place. Watered is a one-tap timestamp save with immediate pending feedback, explicit success/error, duplicate prevention and optional post-save details. Other care actions use a native accessible dialog/bottom sheet.

Marketing: exact concept/user headline “Every plant has a history.” / “Give it an identity.”; CTA Start your collection / Explore the demo; Tap its tag / Know what changed / Care with confidence; A small tag. A lifetime of context.; Know the change. Not just the photograph.; One plant. Or a thousand.; Start a living collection.

Forms, errors, empty states, privacy controls, transfer confirmation and analysis provenance are concrete requested functional continuations in this visual system.

## Intentional departures from concepts

Synthetic demo indication, provider labels and baseline/confidence messaging are necessary truth boundaries. Live database values override raster-concept fixture counts/dates. Do not mimic a phone OS status bar. No stock portrait of a fictional employee; use initials. Buttons use a darker saffron for contrast. Desktop profile adapts to two columns; mobile retains photo→health→care→history. No fabricated testimonials or externally measured performance. Fixed AIDA/randomized layouts/GSAP pinning do not apply to operational screens; marketing uses one restrained line-draw reveal and no forced pinning.

## Primary acceptance paths

Register → workspace → plant → optional first photo → tag URL / printable QR → public passport or authorized profile → watered → persisted timeline → photo upload → durable analysis → explainable health/alerts. Fleet search/filter/table/cards; locations; team role denial; tag replacement and retired URL; owner transfer acceptance preserving history.
